Brake Pedal Pivot

Solidworks TIG Welding

Overview

The team's old brake pivot was manufactured from lasercut steel sheets, which were 1/8th of an inch thick. This meant that it would bend under even very light loads, and that when subjected to real-life braking conditions, it may snap off and put the driver in danger. I was put in charge of designing and manufacturing the new pivot.

What I built

  • The brake pedal was fully designed in Solidworks, because it was critical to run FEA on the part. We wanted to reduce torsion of the part as much as possible, which meant thickening the walls and adding a U-shaped support between the two walls of the pivot.
  • I also designed the bushings and supports that would allow the pedal to pivot, as well as the bolt that would hold all of it together, but unfortunately lost the CAD file and couldn't find screenshots.

Result
