Flyball Governor

Onshape 3D Printing Arduino

Overview

I worked with two groupmates to fulfill the objective of attenuating a non-constant, sinusoidal voltage input motor and have the system output constant voltage.

What I built

  • My group, inspired by James Watt's flyball governor for steam engines, decided to build one ourselves.
  • We aimed to maximize the inertia of the arms and flywheel in order to lessen the effects of the inconsistent voltage input.
